They are used for evaluating network performance, influencing decisions related to handovers, link adaptation, and overall network management. Examples of KPIs include signal-to-interference plus noise ratio (SINR), frequency, synchronization signal block index, modulation and coding scheme, bit-error rate, etc. These KPIs are extracted from devices like CPEs using various methods such as active and passive techniques, data-driven optimization, ML, and AT commands.
